# Feature Proposal: SAIL-PLATFORM-V3.1
## Integrated Digital Platform for Sailing Regattas

### Executive Summary
The goal is to unify the historically fragmented sailing regatta ecosystem into a single, cloud-native architecture. This platform integrates administrative management, scoring, race direction, and real-time telemetry to provide a seamless experience for clubs and sailors.

### Business Objectives
* **BO-01 (Administrative Automation):** Drastically reduce manual club workloads through smart registration and automated financial flows.
* **BO-02 (Sporting Precision):** Implement RTK-level precision and advanced algorithms to ensure fair and accurate racing results.
* **BO-03 (Sustainability):** Support eco-friendly "Platinum" event certifications by using autonomous robotic buoys that do not require anchors.
* **BO-04 (Public Engagement):** Utilize AR and AI-driven content generation to modernize the spectator experience.
